## Description

Run It UP! Dallas is the official app for the biggest run club in Dallas. Founded by Theo Murdaugh, Run It UP! has built a movement around community, consistency, and showing up for yourself one mile at a time. Now the entire experience lives in one app.

CHECK IN. SHOW UP. GET CREDIT.
GPS-verified check-ins at every run so your effort counts. No more screenshots, no more "I was there" — the app knows. Check-in opens 30 minutes before each run and stays live for one hour after, so you never miss your window.

4 WEEKLY RUNS. ZERO EXCUSES.
- Monday — Trinity Groves
- Tuesday — Deep Ellum
- Wednesday — Oak Cliff (Monthly Pop-Up)
- Saturday — Fair Oaks
- Sunday — Levy Plaza

Every run is free. Every run is open to all paces. Walk it, jog it, run it — just show up.

RUN BUDDY — NEVER RUN ALONE
New to the crew? The Run Buddy feature pairs you with an experienced runner matched to your pace group. Whether you are a 7-minute miler or taking your first steps off the couch, your buddy makes sure you feel welcome from day one. No one gets left behind.

EARN BADGES. CLIMB THE LEADERBOARD.
10 collectible badges reward your consistency and milestones — from your first check-in to your 100th run. The community leaderboard ranks runners by streak, total miles, and check-ins so you can chase your personal best or compete with your crew.

COMMUNITY CHAT — STAY CONNECTED
12 channels keep the conversation going between runs. Chat by run day, pace group, or jump into social channels for everything from gear recommendations to post-run food spots. This is your run family — not a group text that never stops.

EVENTS & CALENDAR
Every run, pop-up, social event, and community meetup in one calendar. RSVP, get reminders, and never miss a run again. No more juggling Eventbrite, Google Calendar, and Instagram stories to figure out what is happening this week.

YOUR STATS. YOUR STORY.
Track your runs, streaks, total miles, and badge collection in your personal profile. Watch your progress stack up week after week.

Run It UP! Dallas is 100% free with zero ads. This app was built for the community, by the community. Download it, check in Monday, and find out why people keep coming back.

See you out there. Lace up.
